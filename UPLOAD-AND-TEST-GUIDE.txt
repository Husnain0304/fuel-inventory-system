MEASURES
- On-time: first receipt on/before the release planned-delivery date.
- Fill rate: accepted release quantity divided by released quantity, capped at 100%.
- Receipt accuracy: 100% less the absolute dispatched-versus-accepted variance.
- Claim-free: 100% less claimed liters as a share of received liters.
- Overall: weighted average of available measures only.
- NO DATA means the supplier had no measurable activity in the selected period.
