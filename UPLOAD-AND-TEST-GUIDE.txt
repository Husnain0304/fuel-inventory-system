SUPPLIER MASTER & COMPLIANCE V1

Upload these 7 complete replacement files to the root of the GitHub foundation-v2 branch:

1. app.py
2. supplier_master.py (new file)
3. rbac.py
4. procurement.py
5. storage_operations.py
6. transactions.py
7. driver_app.py

GITHUB
1. Open the fuel-inventory-system repository.
2. Confirm the branch selector says foundation-v2.
3. Select Add file, then Upload files.
4. Drag all 7 files into GitHub together.
5. Commit directly to foundation-v2.

STREAMLIT
1. Wait for automatic deployment.
2. Open the app from Streamlit Community Cloud.
3. If the old menu remains, use Manage app and Reboot once.
4. Do not delete or reset PostgreSQL. Existing suppliers are upgraded automatically.

FIRST TEST
1. Sign in as Administrator or Inventory Manager.
2. Open Supplier Master.
3. Confirm existing suppliers have codes such as SUP-0001.
4. Open Profile & status and complete one existing supplier profile.
5. Save it and confirm the profile remains after refreshing.
6. Open Contacts & products, add a contact and choose approved products.
7. Open Performance & exposure and confirm existing bookings, receipts and claims appear.

BLOCKING TEST
1. Create a temporary supplier or select a supplier that is safe to test.
2. Change its status to BLOCKED.
3. Open Supplier Procurement and confirm it is not offered for a new booking.
4. Open Storage Operations and confirm it is not offered for a new supplier receipt.
5. Return to Supplier Master and change it back to ACTIVE if required.

COMPLIANCE
- EXPIRED: trade licence date is earlier than today.
- EXPIRING: trade licence expires within 30 days.
- VALID: expiry is more than 30 days away.
- BLOCKED/INACTIVE suppliers remain in historical reports and audit records.

EVIDENCE
To attach a supplier licence, contract, tax certificate or bank letter:
1. Open Evidence Centre.
2. Upload the document.
3. Choose Supplier as the linked record type.
4. Enter the numeric Supplier ID shown in Supplier Master.
